- Load 3DBAG (TU Delft, LoD 1.2/2.2 heights) for Rotterdam tiles, estimate floors and gross floor area per building.
- Hexagon `fsi` (floor space index) and `osr` (open space ratio).
- Visual: **Spacemate** scatter (GSI on x, FSI on y, one dot per hexagon, study areas highlighted) after Berghauser Pont & Haupt. This is the standard Dutch density diagram and a good fit for Jacobs' point that density is not the same as overcrowding (ch. 11).
