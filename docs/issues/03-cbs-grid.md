- CBS *vierkantstatistieken* 100 m: population, households, average WOZ value, share of rental housing.
- CBS *Kerncijfers wijken en buurten*: business establishments by sector (a stand-in for jobs), cars per household.
- Metrics: `pop_per_ha`, `woz_spread` (coefficient of variation of WOZ inside a hexagon's neighbourhood). Jacobs' aged-buildings argument is really about a *mix of rents*.
- Hosts: `opendata.cbs.nl`, `datasets.cbs.nl` (currently blocked in the cloud build environment).
